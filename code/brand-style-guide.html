Brand Overview

TestNauti.co is a vibrant, energetic online platform designed to help students conquer their Spanish nautical certification exams, starting with the PER (Patrón de Embarcación de Recreo). Our brand identity captures the excitement of the open sea – bold, flashy, and motivating – while maintaining the professionalism and trust students need for serious exam preparation.


Brand Personality: Energetic • Confident • Fun • Marine-Inspired • Trustworthy • Modern
Target Audience: Aspiring boat captains, adventure seekers, nautical enthusiasts preparing for Spanish maritime certifications
Design Philosophy: "Exciting day at sea" – vibrant blues meet coral sunsets, creating a dynamic visual experience that motivates and energizes

Font Pairing Philosophy

Poppins brings bold confidence and modern energy – perfect for commanding attention in headlines and establishing brand authority.

Quicksand adds a friendly, approachable personality – ideal for secondary headings and interactive elements like buttons, creating warmth without sacrificing professionalism.

Inter provides exceptional readability for body text – crucial for exam questions and long-form content, ensuring students can focus on learning without eye strain.

Visual Direction:

  • Photography Style: Bright, high-contrast images of vibrant seas, modern boats, and sunny coastal scenes
  • Atmosphere: Energetic, optimistic, and aspirational – "exciting day at sea"
  • Avoid: Muted tones, stormy weather, overly technical imagery, corporate stock photos
  • Embrace: Motion, energy, success moments, clear blue waters, golden hour lighting
  • Textures: Subtle wave patterns, light ripples, gradient overlays
  • Composition: Dynamic angles, horizon lines, sense of movement and progress

Dark Mode Guidelines:

  • Maintain Vibrancy: Keep colors bold and saturated – avoid washing out the energy
  • Enhance Glow: Increase shadow opacity and glow effects for depth
  • Text Contrast: White (#FFFFFF) for headings, rgba(255,255,255,0.85) for body
  • Gradients: Same gradients work beautifully in dark mode with enhanced shadows
  • Focus: Use brighter versions of vibrant blue for enhanced visibility

Usage Guidelines & Best Practices

✅ DO

  • Use vibrant blues as the dominant color
  • Apply gradients generously for energy
  • Keep corners rounded (12-20px)
  • Add hover effects with movement
  • Use bold, confident typography
  • Include maritime symbols tastefully
  • Maintain high contrast for readability
  • Celebrate achievements with warm accents

❌ DON'T

  • Use muted or desaturated colors
  • Create flat, static designs
  • Use sharp, harsh corners
  • Overcrowd with too many colors at once
  • Use thin, delicate typography
  • Add nautical clichés excessively
  • Sacrifice readability for style
  • Make the design feel corporate or boring
